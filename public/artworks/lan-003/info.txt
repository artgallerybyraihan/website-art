Title: The Volcanic Shore
Artist: Condro Puspitosari
Category: landscape
Medium: Oil on Linen
Size: 90 × 130 cm (35.4" × 51.2")
Year: 2023
Status: available
Description: Raw power meets serenity volcanic black sands meet the untamed ocean under skies heavy with atmosphere and the promise of storm.
LongDescription:
"The Volcanic Shore" is the largest work in Condro Puspitosari's current collection, and it commands attention. Painted on fine Belgian linen, this piece captures the dramatic meeting point between volcanic black sands and the untamed Indonesian ocean.

The sky dominates the composition heavy with atmosphere, charged with the energy of an approaching storm. Yet there is serenity in the scene too, a stillness in the way the dark water meets the darker shore. The textured impasto technique used for the waves gives them a physical presence that flat paint cannot achieve.

This painting was inspired by the southern coast of Java, where nature's raw power is on constant display a landscape that humbles and inspires in equal measure.
