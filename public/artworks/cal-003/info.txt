LongDescription:
"Letters from the Earth" is an exploration of language as something organic born not from intellect alone, but from the same earth that sustains us. Using earth pigments and walnut ink on handmade paper, this work reimagines ancient Arabic letterforms through layers of translucent washes and deliberate mark-making.

The warm, muted palette evokes the tones of soil, stone, and bark materials that have carried human stories for millennia. Each layer of pigment was applied and allowed to dry before the next, building a depth that can only be appreciated in person.

This piece speaks to those who feel a connection between language and landscape, between the written word and the natural world that inspired it.
