Title: Morning Mist Over the Valley
Artist: Condro Puspitosari
Category: landscape
Medium: Oil on Canvas
Size: 80 × 120 cm (31.5" × 47.2")
Year: 2024
Status: available
Description: Golden light breaks through veils of mist, revealing the lush valleys of Java in a moment of quiet revelation. The landscape breathes.
LongDescription:
"Morning Mist Over the Valley" captures a fleeting moment in the Indonesian highlands that brief window when dawn light pierces through the morning mist, illuminating the lush valleys of Java in golden warmth. The landscape seems to breathe, alive with moisture and light.

Condro Puspitosari worked on this canvas over several weeks, building layer upon layer of oil paint to achieve the atmospheric depth that makes the mist feel tangible. The technique of layered glazes thin, translucent applications of color over dried layers creates a luminosity that cannot be achieved any other way.

This painting is not a photograph of a place. It is a feeling made visible the sensation of standing in silence as the world slowly reveals itself at dawn.
