Title: Tides of Devotion
Artist: Raihan Mohammad
Category: calligraphy
Medium: Indigo Ink & Silver on Canvas
Size: 70 × 100 cm (27.6" × 39.4")
Year: 2024
Status: collected
Description: An oceanic meditation rendered in deep indigo each stroke a wave of intention rising and falling in rhythmic devotion.
LongDescription:
"Tides of Devotion" draws from the imagery of the sea vast, rhythmic, and deeply spiritual. Rendered in deep indigo ink and silver on canvas, the calligraphic forms rise and fall like waves, creating a visual rhythm that mirrors the ebb and flow of prayer and contemplation.

The indigo palette was chosen for its depth and its historical connection to sacred textiles and manuscripts. Silver accents emerge from the darker layers, suggesting moonlight on water moments of clarity within the depths of devotion.

Every brushstroke in this work was made with intention. There are no corrections, no do-overs. What you see is the honest record of a moment of creation unrepeatable and singular.
