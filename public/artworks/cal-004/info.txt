Title: The Still Hours
Artist: Raihan Mohammad
Category: calligraphy
Medium: Sumi Ink & Gouache on Washi
Size: 45 × 60 cm (17.7" × 23.6")
Year: 2024
Status: available
Description: Created in the quietest moments before dawn an intimate work where brushwork meets breath, each stroke a passage of time made visible.
LongDescription:
"The Still Hours" was created during the pre-dawn hours that liminal space between night and day when the world is at its quietest and the mind is most receptive. Working with sumi ink and gouache on delicate washi paper, this piece captures the intimacy of those solitary moments.

The composition is deliberately sparse, allowing negative space to carry as much weight as the brushstrokes themselves. Each mark was made in a single breath, without hesitation a practice rooted in the meditative traditions of East Asian calligraphy, here applied to Arabic letterforms.

This is perhaps the most personal work in the collection a quiet conversation between the artist and the page, preserved in ink for those who know how to listen.
